# **Democratic Tech Fund**

Our digital lives run on infrastructure we don’t own. The clouds where we work, the platforms where we organise, the social networks where we speak and the tools that sustain our communities are increasingly controlled by a handful of corporations.

## **Democratic Tech Fund (DTF) exists to change that.**

DTF is a growing federation of cooperatives, communities and public-interest organisations building **contra-infrastructure**: technology, knowledge and funding that communities can own, govern and sustain together.

We don’t need to start from zero. Alternatives already exist. DTF connects them, strengthens them and helps them travel.

## **BUILD → LEARN → FUND → FEDERATE**

**Build** Cooperative, open and community-governed digital infrastructure.

**Learn** Shared knowledge, peer learning and support for communities moving towards digital autonomy.

**Fund** Collective funding mechanisms that turn community priorities into viable projects.

**Federate** Shared infrastructure, resources and solidarity across communities, cities and borders.

DTF is not only about replacing one piece of software with another. It is about reclaiming the infrastructures through which we **live, organise, care, remember and act together**.

# **DTF BY GOTEO**

**Crowdfunding the infrastructure we want to live with.**

**DTF by Goteo is the crowdfunding arm of the Democratic Tech Fund.**

Built with Fundación Platoniq and Goteo, it turns DTF’s mission into a practical funding pipeline: communities identify needs, projects emerge and are validated, citizens back them through crowdfunding, and institutions and funding partners can **match the crowd**.

Instead of deciding behind closed doors what deserves funding, we use crowdfunding as a form of **distributed decision-making and civic validation**.

## **COMMUNITY → PROJECT → CROWD → MATCH → COMMONS**

A community identifies a need. A project proposes an alternative. The crowd decides to support it. Funders multiply that support through match-funding. Resources flow into infrastructure that creates collective value.

Goteo brings more than fifteen years of experience in civic crowdfunding, with **over €25 million mobilised**, a community of more than **400,000 people**, and thousands of commons-oriented initiatives supported.

**FROM FUNDING PROJECTS TO BUILDING ECOSYSTEMS**

DTF by Goteo organises funding around thematic programmes or **Shelves**: curated spaces where communities, organisations and funders can gather projects around a shared challenge.

A Shelf can support anything from community clouds and federated social media to cooperative platforms, democratic AI, collective archives and technologies that sustain **life and activism**.

Organisations can also become **Shelf Stewards**: bring a community, define a challenge and contribute a funding pool. DTF and its partners can combine these resources with crowdfunding and match-funding so that every contribution helps unlock more collective investment.
